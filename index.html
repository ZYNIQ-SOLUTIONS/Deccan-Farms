Hygiene & Cleanroom FAQ

How Deccan Farms ensures 100% pure, unadulterated freshness.

How long do pre-cut veggies and salads stay fresh?

Because we wash in ozonated RO water and cold-cut at 18°C, our pre-cut vegetables stay crisp for 3–4 days and fresh fruit/salad bowls for 48 hours when refrigerated at 4°C.

Do you add chemical dips, preservatives or sulfites?

Zero. We never use chlorine, sulfite dips, or artificial citrates. Freshness is preserved purely through ozone oxidation, moisture spin drying, and breathable barrier punnets.

How do delivery slots work in Pune?

We run two dedicated chilled temperature-controlled delivery vans across Pune every single day: Morning (7 AM – 9 AM) and Evening (5 PM – 7 PM).
